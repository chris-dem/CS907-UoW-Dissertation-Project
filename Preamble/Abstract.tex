
\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}
\vspace{1cm}

\large
In the world of combinatorics, one should always seek the combinatorial
interpretation of numbers. Such discoveries open the world to many interesting
relations and structures which can bring forward groundbreaking results
This sentiment has sparked a movement where researchers have tried to answer
that question with the help of counting complexity 
\cite{pak_WhatCombinatorialInterpretation_2022,ikenmeyer_WhatWhatNot_2022}.

In our current project we focused around combinatorial interpretations 
of $\textbf{PPAD}-1$ class with the help of the \textit{PureCircuit} problem.
We started by demonstrating how one can imbue \textit{hazard-free} logic into \textbf{PPAD}.
We then focused on the topological interpretation of unbalanced digraphs and 
their potential future reductions with \textit{PureCircuit}. Lastly we analysed,
deeper the effect of multi-source graphs and their combinatorial classes that may blossom.

Moreover with our project we are outlining an application that is able to visualise \textit{PureCircuit}
instances and validate. In addition, we were able to demonstrate the usage of metaheuristic
algorithms to find solutions. Lastly we built our own specialised backtracking algorithm
for enumerating through solutions in \textit{PureCircuit} graphs  and demonstrated substantial
improvements over the traditional brute force algorithm.
